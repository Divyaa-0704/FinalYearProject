ANIMAL ADOPTION - KANHA NATIONAL PARK

Kanha National Park provides an opportunity to adopt animals and contribute to their care. By adopting an animal, you help support its food, medical needs, and habitat maintenance for a life time.

Adopt a majestic Bengal tiger, a graceful barasingha, or other wildlife species unique to Kanha National Park. Your adoption helps in conservation efforts and ensures the well-being of these incredible animals.

Kanha National Park ensures a smooth adoption process and you can choose which animal you want to adopt based on the availability of adoption.
